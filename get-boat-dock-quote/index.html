How to order

LockDry® decking in three easy steps

Ordering decking on its own — for a dock, pier, boardwalk or walkway — takes a sketch, a few details, and an email.

Step 1 — Sketch

Sketch the perimeter

Download our Decking Design Form. Fill it out online, or print it and sketch the outer perimeter of your boat dock, pier, boardwalk or anywhere the decking will be applied. Be sure to add dimensions to any fingers, slips or platforms.

To make things more streamlined, add a drawing scale. That ensures you receive an accurate quote from our design and sales teams.

You can also give us a call and we will be more than happy to fill out your design form for you over the phone.

Step 2 — Detail

Add colors, styles and notes

Choose from one of our various decking styles and colors. Color choices apply to LockDry® aluminum marine decking.

For stairs, include the total number of steps as well as the step width and length. If you know your drawing scale, note it in the box above the design grid. Include any and all special requirements you need us to consider — it eliminates confusion in the quoting process.

See the decking colors→

Step 3 — Send

Send it back

The third and final step is simple. Add your contact and shipping information and send the form back to us by fax or email.

What happens next

After you send it

Turnaround, payment and lead times, so there are no surprises.

  • Quote turnaround

    Once your design form is sent, we send a confirmation by email so you know we received it. An accurate quote is returned within 48 hours. If you do not hear from us, give us a call at 1-800-711-1785 to make sure everything went through.

  • Payment methods

    We accept all major credit cards, wire transfers and checks (all checks must clear before shipment). If you are paying by credit card, please fill out and sign the credit card authorization form sent with your quote.

  • Lead times

    Once you have looked over your quote, sign it and fax or email it back, and we will begin preparing your order. Lead times officially begin once we have a signed quote. For current lead times on standard and special orders, contact us directly.
