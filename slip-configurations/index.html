Slip Configurations

Single or double slips, side entry, a third slip for the PWCs — the layout follows the boats you own and the water you have.

Get a Quote Browse dock styles

Layout

Start with the boats

Slip size and arrangement come from what you keep on the water, not the other way round.

Slip width and length are built to the boat, with room to board comfortably and bumpers where the hull comes close. Side entry suits a narrow lot or a shoreline that runs the wrong way.

A slip fill-in turns part of a slip into shaded deck for PWC parking or seating, and can come out again when the boat changes.

Configurations

Common arrangements

Mix and match — every style we build is available in each of these.

  • Single slip

    One boat, with walkway on one or both sides.

  • Double slip

    Two boats side by side under one roof.

  • Triple and quad

    Three or more slips, with the walkways to serve them.

  • Side entry

    The boat enters from the side, for a narrow lot or a tight approach.

  • Slip fill-in

    Deck over part of a slip for PWC parking or shaded seating.

  • Custom

    L-shaped, H-shaped and horizontal layouts built to the shoreline.
