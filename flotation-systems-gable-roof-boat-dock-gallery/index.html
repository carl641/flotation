Gable roof boat docks

The most economical covered dock we build — a classic peaked roofline over marine-grade aluminum, with a limited lifetime warranty.

Every dock in this gallery started as a conversation about one waterfront: its depth, its elevation, and how the family wanted to use the water.

More about gable roof docks→
  • Single, double, side entry, and custom multi-slip layouts
  • 26-gauge commercial high-rib roof metal
  • Raised decorative ridge and Lodge package roof designs
  • LockDry® aluminum or WearDeck composite decking
  • Spud pole, cable/winch, and follow-the-water anchoring
  • Full, half, and trash deflector swim decks
  • Slip fill-ins for protected PWC parking
  • Dock lockers, closets, benches, and bumpers
  • Aquastair swim ladders and boat lifts
