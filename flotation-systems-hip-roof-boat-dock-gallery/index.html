Hip roof boat docks

Four sloping roof planes over marine-grade aluminum — our most popular covered dock, with a limited lifetime warranty.

Every dock in this gallery started as a conversation about one waterfront: its depth, its elevation, and how the family wanted to use the water.

More about hip roof docks→
  • Single, double, triple, and side entry layouts
  • “L” and “H” shaped and custom roof plans
  • Beaumont Series arched braces
  • Cupolas, weathervanes, and decorative ridges
  • LockDry® aluminum or WearDeck composite decking
  • Spud pole, cable/winch, and piling box anchoring
  • Full, half, and quarter-length swim decks
  • Dock lockers, closets, and storage rooms
  • Aquastair swim ladders, boat lifts, and PWC ports
