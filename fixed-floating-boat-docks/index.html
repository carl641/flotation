Dock Styles

Fixed & Floating Docks

How your dock meets the water. Fixed to pilings, or floating with the level — every style we build comes both ways.

Get a Quote Fixed & stationary gallery

Two ways to anchor the same dock

Flotation Systems offers both fixed and floating dock systems: fixed systems for steel, concrete or wood pilings, and floating systems with a wide variety of anchoring styles.

Anchoring is the first decision, not the last one. It is set by your water — how far it moves through the year, how deep it runs at the dock, and what the bottom will hold.

Every dock style we build — sundeck, sundeck combo, gable roof, hip roof, piers and platforms — is available fixed or floating.

Materials & methods

Fixed and floating, side by side

Both are handcrafted from strong, corrosion-resistant aluminum and engineered with stabilizing WAVEPro technology.

  • Fixed to pilings

    Fixed docks are anchored firmly to steel, concrete, or wood pilings — ideal for water with little tidal or seasonal water level variation.

  • Overhead lifts

    Fixed docks support the installation of overhead watercraft lifts, which floating systems cannot carry.

  • Floating frame

    Floating systems use a rigid, heavy-duty extruded aluminum frame that gives the walking surface a solid, stable base.

  • EPS billets

    Foam-filled, rotationally molded polyethylene encasements from nationally recognized manufacturers, unaffected by salt water and virtually all chemicals.

  • USACE compliant

    Billets meet or exceed all U.S. Army Corps of Engineers regulations.

  • Stainless attachment

    Billets attach to the mainframe with 3/8" stainless steel bolts and 1" stainless steel fender washers, secured with nylon lock nuts.

Configurations

Choosing between them

The right answer is usually written into your shoreline already.

Image placeholder Anchoring detail — choose fixed when

Choose fixed when

Your water level holds steady through the year, the depth at the dock is manageable for pilings, and you want an overhead boat lift.

Image placeholder Anchoring detail — choose floating when

Choose floating when

Your lake or river swings through the season, you take heavy wakes, or the bottom will not take a piling.

Image placeholder Anchoring detail — deep water anchoring

Deep water anchoring

In water deeper than 10 feet, floating sections are held by pilings with custom piling loops, or by a cable and anchor system.
