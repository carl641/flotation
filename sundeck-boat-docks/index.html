Sundeck Boat Docks

More space to do what you love. Double the space of a single-tier pier and platform — a full upper deck for sun, views, and everything you do on the water.

Get a Quote Sundeck gallery

Twice the dock, on the same footprint

Traditional sundeck designs offer double the space of a single-tier dock pier and platform.

High-quality Flotation Systems sundecks feature LockDry® aluminum decking for weatherproof security of the lower dock, powder-coated aluminum picket fencing for beauty and safety, and elegant, space-saving cantilevered stairs.

Upstairs is for sun, dinners, and the long view down the lake. Downstairs stays dry and usable — the same square footage doing two jobs at once.

Sundeck boat docks add more than a chapter to your life on the water. They add a whole other story. It begins with the ultimate in secure, protected docking — from there, you write the tale of relaxation and entertainment.

Our sundeck designs welcome a unique selection of enhancements, including swim platforms, shades, seating options, and much more. The options are many, but the choice is simple: Flotation Systems sundecks take waterfront style to a new level.

Materials & methods

Built into every sundeck

No rust, no rot, and a limited lifetime warranty on residential docks installed by Flotation Systems or an authorized dealer.

  • LockDry® aluminum decking

    Interlocking watertight planks channel rain and spray away through a concealed gutter system, so the lower dock stays dry and usable.

  • Picket fencing

    Powder-coated aluminum picket fencing runs the upper deck for beauty and safety, in colors matched to your decking and roof.

  • Cantilevered stairs

    Elegant, space-saving stairs cantilever over the water — your dock surface stays clear and the footprint stays small.

  • Marine-grade aluminum

    Corrosion-resistant aluminum framing and stainless steel hardware throughout. Nothing to splinter, rot, or refinish.

  • WAVEPro™ technology

    Every dock is engineered with stabilizing WAVEPro construction for solid footing in harsh weather and heavy water.

  • Limited lifetime warranty

    Residential docks installed by Flotation Systems or an authorized dealer carry a limited lifetime warranty.

Configurations

Ways to configure a sundeck

Single or double slips, open or partly covered — the deck above stays yours to plan.

Single slip sundeck

One full-size slip below, full deck above. The most compact way to get a second story on the water.

Double slip sundeck

Traditional side-by-side slips for two full-size watercraft, with the upper deck spanning both.

Slip and offset PWC

One large slip plus an offset personal watercraft slip, which opens a shaded seating area with a view of open water.
