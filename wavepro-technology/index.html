The idea

Wave Attentive Versatility Engineered

Developed to skillfully master the waves and stand strong against the forces of nature.

Through state-of-the-art engineering and meticulous construction practices, our docks are built not only to survive but to thrive when conditions get rough.

Flotation Systems docks define the dock industry standards for beauty in design, construction, durability and strength — for a lifetime of enjoyment.

How it works

What the engineering does

Three things working together, on every dock we build.

  • Wave attenuation

    Engineered assemblies absorb and dampen wake energy instead of passing it into the dock.

  • Marine-grade aluminum

    Corrosion-resistant structural alloy that will not rust or rot, season after season.

  • Stainless steel hardware

    Precision-engineered connections that hold tight through years of movement.

  • 8-inch framing

    Heavy-weight framing carries the load and keeps the deck flat.

  • Sealed flotation

    Foam-filled billets that meet or exceed U.S. Army Corps of Engineers regulations.

  • High traffic ready

    Built for busy lakes, heavy wakes and the weather that comes with them.
