WAVEPro™ Technology

Wave Attentive Versatility Engineered — developed to skillfully master the waves and stand strong against the forces of nature.

Why we built it

Looking good is great. Standing strong is even better.

Busier waterways and the forces of nature require a dock built to not only survive, but to thrive when conditions get rough. That is why we developed WAVEPro™ technology.

Every Flotation Systems dock features innovative, stabilizing WAVEPro™ technology. Through state-of-the-art engineering and meticulous construction practices, our docks are built to endure year after year, in demanding weather and water conditions.

The difference

What sets a WAVEPro-equipped dock apart from the rest?

Six things, on every dock we build — not upgrades on an order form.

Engineer-designed components

Strong at every point

Standard in every Flotation Systems dock is a distinctive combination of 10″ heavy gauge E-channel and reinforced corner sleeves with double gussets.

The 10″ E-channel is integrated throughout the entire substructure.

Particular points of tension and stress receive mainframe corner sleeves and double diagonal gusset reinforcement.

Our engineers have worked to ensure that your dock is strong at every point — where other docks are not.

Custom materials and construction

Moldings and extrusions we design ourselves

We design our own custom moldings, extrusions and finishing products for key dock components, incorporated throughout the dock system to better fit each dock to its environment.

Our experienced in-house design team engineers use 3-D CAD software for a more exacting fit and consistent components. This rigorous engineering is why every dock installed by an authorized Flotation Systems dealer carries our industry-leading limited lifetime warranty.

Powder coat finishes for decking, roofing and railings provide highly resistant protection against all forms of wear, including scratching, fading, chipping and corrosion.

Upper decks feature gapless, waterproof LockDry® aluminum marine decking, one of the highest consumer-rated marine decking. Its SuperDurable™ powder coat finish is applied to specification AAMA 2604 — higher than the typical AAMA 2603 spec in the market — with three to four times the thickness of wet, baked-on paints and high impact resistance. It will not peel off like enamel finishes, and is extremely durable to the elements and resistant to most chemicals and solvents.

All aluminum powder coated railings are laboratory tested and building code approved. Other railing design options are available upon request.

Heavy-duty roof structure

True 4-inch posts, welded into the mainframe

We use true 4″ roof support posts with radius corners incorporated within the 10″ E-channel lower mainframe.

This creates an unmatched, welded double gusset assembly to better withstand extreme marine conditions, and enables us to produce one of the best engineered roof systems in the dock industry. Roof post bushing reduces noise by eliminating metal-to-metal friction.

“Box” channel post and truss roof assembly creates a strong, reliable connection designed for the “long haul” life of your dock.

Each FSI corner roof post is anchored to the mainframe using 5″ high-quality stainless steel hex bolts and nylon lock nuts, for maximum anchorage and a secure connection. All other interior roof supports are secured with double 3/8″ x 5″ stainless steel bolts at both the top and bottom of the posts.

Diagonal bracing

The solid core of a rigid dock

Quality diagonal bracing is essential for dock rigidity — the solid core required to maintain a dock’s structural integrity.

We brace every dock with sturdy, radius-cornered 4″ x 4″ members.

To allow for a more user-friendly dock, diagonal reinforcement bracing is located overhead at high stress areas, corners and connection points. Bracing in these areas assists in maintaining rigidity and stability in rough water or windy conditions. There is no substitute for diagonal bracing.

Other manufacturers call this overbuilt. We will not build a dock without it.

Ramps, gangways and bridges

An arched truss and a true piano hinge

Our arched bridge truss design is an all-aluminum construction in 5′ to 80′ standard single span lengths.

Reinforced ramp bracing is available depending on application and design.

A true “piano hinge” ramp connection uses a marine-grade, solid aluminum UHMW bushing-encased hinge pin through aluminum 3/8″ wall pipe, incorporated in a full-length track system welded to the mainframe channel. It is ideal for upper or lower level ramp attachments, and double diagonal gussets reinforce high stress locations.

Boat dock floats

The ultimate in durable dock flotation and support

The very foundation of every dock is a quality float, and ours set the standard for performance and durability. This is no place to cut corners, and we do not.

Our EPS billets are manufactured by nationally recognized companies. These foam-filled, rotomolded polyethylene encasements meet or exceed all U.S. Army Corps of Engineers regulations, are unaffected by salt water and virtually all chemicals, and are covered by our exclusive limited lifetime warranty.

Flotation billets are attached to the mainframe using 3/8″ stainless steel bolts and 1″ stainless steel fender washers, secured with nylon lock nuts. The result: floats that remain secure under severe conditions, without separation or movement.
