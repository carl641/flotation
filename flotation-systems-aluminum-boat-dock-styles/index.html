Anchoring

Fixed or floating — every style, both ways

Flotation Systems offers both fixed and floating dock systems: fixed systems for steel, concrete or wood pilings, and floating systems with a wide variety of anchoring styles.

Fixed docks are ideal for water with little tidal or seasonal water level variation, and they support the installation of overhead watercraft lifts. Floating docks are the adaptive solution for diverse shorelines, resting on tough sealed floats and riding out large changes in water level and wakes with ease.

Compare fixed and floating→

  • Custom piling loops
  • Cable and anchor systems
  • Foam-filled EPS billets
  • Overhead lifts on fixed docks
  • Drive-on lifts on floating docks
  • LockDry® watertight decking
