Pier & platform docks

Fixed and floating piers, walkways, swim and fishing platforms — built from marine-grade aluminum with a limited lifetime warranty.

Every platform in this gallery started as a conversation about one waterfront: its depth, its elevation, and how the family wanted to use the water.

More about piers & platforms→
  • 6 ft. and custom walkway widths
  • Powder-coated custom handrails
  • ADA compliant walkways
  • LockDry® or open aluminum decking
  • Spud pole, cable/winch, and wheel-jack anchoring
  • Gable and hip shade structures
  • Aquastair swim ladders
  • Dock lockers, benches, and bumpers
  • Fixed or floating anchoring
