Our story

Three decades of all-aluminum engineering

Jimmy Conn founded Flotation Systems in Cullman, Alabama in 1992, starting with the first residential all-aluminum boat dock system.

Every dock still carries that engineering-first approach: marine-grade aluminum framing, stainless steel hardware, and construction that will not rust or rot.

What began as a single residential system now covers sundecks, covered slips, piers and platforms, ADA-compliant access, and full commercial marinas — fixed or floating, on lakes and rivers nationwide.

How we work

The same commitments on a single residential slip and on a hundred-slip marina.

  • Built in our own shop

    Designed, engineered, and fabricated in Cullman, Alabama — not assembled from someone else’s parts.

  • Aluminum, always

    All-aluminum construction from the first dock we built. Nothing to rust, rot, splinter, or refinish.

  • Engineered for rough water

    WAVEPro™ technology is standard on every dock, not an upgrade on the order form.

  • Installed locally

    An authorized dealer network that knows your water levels, shoreline rules, and permitting.

  • Customized to you

    A vast array of styles, colors, and accessories, specified around how you actually use the water.

  • Backed for a lifetime

    A limited lifetime warranty on residential docks installed by us or an authorized dealer.
