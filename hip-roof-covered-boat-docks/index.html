Hip Roof Boat Docks

Style meets functionality. Clean lines that add stunning design to your shoreline, placing you and your watercraft stylishly under the sun and stars.

Get a Quote Hip roof gallery

A safe haven from sun, rain, and wind

Superior materials and construction create a safe haven from sun, rain, and wind.

Winds are kept in check by the hip roof's vented top ridge and corner seams — the four-sided roof gives the weather nothing flat to push against.

A wide range of options and accessories create almost endless covered boat dock designs to fit your lifestyle. Choose your roof color and add removable slip fill-ins for more shaded living space.

Modern, yet classic. Every detail reveals our legendary design excellence, all created for carefree waterfront living — stylish options, exceptional function, our hip roof designs are a perfect fit.

Materials & methods

Built into every hip roof dock

All Flotation Systems dock roofs are formed from 26 gauge aluminum.

  • Vented top ridge

    The vented ridge and corner seams let wind pass through instead of lifting the roof.

  • 26-gauge aluminum roof

    Every roof is formed from 26 gauge aluminum with a durable Valspar coating.

  • 40-year color retention

    The Valspar finish carries a 40 year color retention warranty.

  • Decorative cupola

    An optional cupola adds flair at the peak — louvers or four-pane glass windows, with or without a weather vane.

  • Removable slip fill-ins

    Fill in a slip to add shaded living space, and pull it back out when you need the water.

  • WAVEPro™ technology

    Stabilizing construction engineered for heavy wakes and rough weather.

Configurations

Slip configurations

Single or double slips, with room for a personal watercraft alongside.

Single slip

One full-size slip under a four-sided roof, the cleanest silhouette on the water.

Double slip

Side-by-side slips for two full-size watercraft under one hip roof.

Slip and offset PWC

A large slip with an offset PWC slip, opening a shaded seating area facing open water.
