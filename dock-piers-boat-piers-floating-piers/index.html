Materials & methods

Built into every pier and platform

The same marine-grade aluminum and stainless steel hardware as the covered docks.

  • Fixed or floating

    Fixed piers anchor to pilings; floating piers ride sealed billets through changing water levels.

  • 6-foot walkways

    Wide walkways with room to carry gear, with narrower widths available where the shoreline is tight.

  • Custom handrails

    Powder-coated aluminum handrails in your color, built to the run of your pier.

  • ADA compliant access

    ADA compliant walkways and platforms, including private aluminum fishing piers.

  • LockDry® or open decking

    Choose watertight LockDry® decking or an open aluminum plank surface.

  • Marine-grade aluminum

    Corrosion-resistant aluminum with stainless steel hardware. Nothing to rust or rot.

Configurations

Piers and platforms

Get from your property to your water, at whatever elevation the shoreline gives you.

Walkway piers

Straight or angled runs from the bank out to the dock, with handrails where you want them.

Swim and fishing platforms

Open platforms sized for whatever you do off the end of them.

Ramps and gangways

Short beach ramps through graceful stair and gangway combinations for any elevation.
