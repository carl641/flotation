What it is

What is LockDry® aluminum marine decking?

Simply put, it is revolutionary.

LockDry’s patented locking seal and continuous gutter technology provides a gapless floor decking and roof covering with single-layer construction. It is the most superior decking material in the marine industry — unlike composite or wood, which over time become brittle and splinter, retain heat, and require constant maintenance.

LockDry’s maintenance-free design eliminates the need for painting, staining or waterproofing, and is virtually stain and mar-resistant.

The idea

Imagine a second story deck that completely waterproofs the space below

Interlocking planks channel rain and spray into a concealed gutter system, so the deck above keeps the space beneath it dry and usable.

There are no exposed fasteners to work loose, no gaps between planks, and nothing to splinter, rot or need refinishing.

The system

Three profiles, one continuous floor

Planks lock to each other across the run, starting and finishing flush at the edges.

Starter

The first plank in the run, closing the edge of the deck and starting the locking seal.

Main

The full-width plank that repeats across the span, each one locking into the last.

Finisher

The closing plank, finishing the run flush against the opposite edge.

Decking specs

Material
6005-T5 aluminum, 35,000 psi yield strength
Size
6″ width, 1″ thick. Standard lengths 4′ to 32′, longer available upon request
Finish
Textured high-density polyester powder coat, meets or exceeds AAMA 2604
Fire rating
Class A — flame spread value “0”
Span
12″–24″ on center recommended, 36″ on center maximum
Live load
Tested to 240 PSF on 36″ centers
