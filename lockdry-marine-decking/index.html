The decking

Dry underneath, grip on top

LockDry® is exceptional decking in every sense: strong, watertight, and non-slip textured.

The interlocking planks shed water to the edge rather than through the deck, so the space below a sundeck stays usable and dry.

A robust powder coated finish gives it lifetime durability that far exceeds any other materials, in a textured, non-skid surface that stays comfortable underfoot.

Why it holds up

Built for the water

The reasons it goes on every dock rather than as an upgrade.

  • Watertight

    Water runs off the deck, not through it.

  • Non-slip texture

    A textured, non-skid finish, wet or dry.

  • Powder coated

    A high-density polyester finish up to three times thicker than conventional finishes.

  • Four colors

    Buckskin, Granite Gray, Silverstone and Hearthstone.

  • No rot, no splinters

    Aluminum, so there is nothing to rot, warp or sand.

  • Lifetime durability

    It outlasts the alternatives, season after season.
