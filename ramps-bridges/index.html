Access

Your options are almost limitless

We custom-build each entry to fit your shoreline profile.

Options include top- or bottom-level entry to sundeck boat docks, and ramp-to-stairs entry for docks that sit far below the land.

Our aluminum gangways are crafted to perfectly reflect the design of your dock, so the route out to the water looks like part of the dock rather than an afterthought.

Engineering

An arched bridge truss, in all aluminum

All-aluminum construction with a high strength-to-weight ratio, engineered to span.

The live load capacity is from 30 up to 50 pounds per square foot, and P.E.-approved drawings are available for compliance verification.

For strength in all conditions, a full-length stainless steel hinge pin with 3/8″ aluminum wall exterior and reinforcing gussets at all corners and connections are standard.

Specification

What comes standard

The numbers behind the span, and the choices you make on top of them.

  • Arched bridge truss

    An arched truss design in all-aluminum construction, with a high strength-to-weight ratio.

  • 30–50 lbs. per sq. ft.

    Live load capacity from 30 up to 50 pounds per square foot.

  • P.E.-approved drawings

    Available for compliance verification on permitted and commercial projects.

  • Full-length hinge pin

    A stainless steel hinge pin running the full length, with a 3/8″ aluminum wall exterior.

  • Reinforcing gussets

    Standard at all corners and connections, for strength in all conditions.

  • 5 to 80 foot spans

    Gangways span from 5 feet to 80 feet, with supporting undercarriage for runs greater than 60 feet.

  • Your choice of width

    Ramps can be made in a variety of widths, with picket railings and lockable gates available.

  • Fully ADA-compliant

    Each of our aluminum dock ramps can be built to full ADA compliance.
