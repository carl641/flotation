Gable Roof Boat Docks

Classic looks and solid performance. Relax and play under a canopy of protection — your boat, your possessions, your guests, all stylishly secure.

Get a Quote Gable roof gallery

The most economical covered dock we build

The gable roof boat dock is the most economical of our covered boat docks — and it receives the same attention to detail as every other dock we build.

Stainless steel hardware, 8-inch heavy-weight framing, 26-gauge commercial high-rib roof metal, and a 30-year color retention warranty come standard.

A classic peaked roofline shelters the boat and the dock beneath it, and a raised decorative ridge and a full range of roof colors let it reflect your own style.

Simple. Timeless. Accessories like stairs, swim platforms, and slip fill-ins give you more space and functionality — and with everything our covered boat docks have to offer, you’ll be as happy docked as you are on the water.

Materials & methods

Built into every gable roof dock

Economical is not the same as basic. The specification below is standard, not upgraded.

  • 8-inch heavy-weight framing

    Heavy-weight aluminum framing carries the roof and the dock without flex, season after season.

  • 26-gauge high-rib roof metal

    Commercial high-rib roof metal sheds water and stands up to wind and hail.

  • 30-year color retention

    The roof finish carries a 30-year color retention warranty, so the dock still looks new in a decade.

  • Stainless steel hardware

    Stainless fasteners throughout — nothing to rust into the aluminum below it.

  • Raised decorative ridge

    An optional raised ridge lifts the roof line and vents the peak.

  • WAVEPro™ technology

    Stabilizing construction engineered for the strongest wakes and toughest weather.

Configurations

Slip configurations

Gable roof docks are available with single or double boat slips.

Single slip

One full-size slip under roof. The most economical way to get your boat covered.

Double slip

Traditional side-by-side slips, ideal for two full-size watercraft.

Slip and offset PWC

One large slip with an offset personal watercraft slip, opening a shaded seating area with a view of open water.

Options

Options and accessories

Customization options include a variety of roof colors, a raised decorative ridge, and more to reflect individual style. Stairs, swim platforms, and slip fill-ins give you more space and functionality.
