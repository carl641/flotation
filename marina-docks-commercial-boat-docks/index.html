Custom commercial marinas

A Flotation Systems marina is the centerpiece of your docking operation.

Every commercial and marina dock we build comes with our exclusive WAVEPro design and construction, engineered to handle the strongest wakes, the most traffic, and the toughest weather with ease.

Whether it is a large-scale commercial operation or a smaller community dock, the system is laid out around how your slips actually get used — and built in aluminum, so decades of traffic and weather do not eat it.

Materials & methods

Built into every commercial system

Engineered for the load a private dock never sees.

  • Exclusive WAVEPro™

    WAVEPro design and construction handles the strongest wakes, the most traffic, and the toughest weather.

  • Marine-grade aluminum

    Extruded aluminum framing withstands harsh weather and the constant flow of foot traffic and golf carts.

  • Commercial flotation

    Foam-filled, rotomolded encasements meeting or exceeding U.S. Army Corps of Engineers regulations.

  • Configurable layouts

    Straight, T, L, U, and slip-style layouts, with finger docks to optimize berthing.

  • ADA compliant access

    ADA compliant walkways and gangways where your operation needs them.

  • Low maintenance

    Aluminum and stainless steel do not rust or rot, so annual upkeep stays close to nothing.
