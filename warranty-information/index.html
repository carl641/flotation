The best dock warranty you’ll probably never need

Flotation Systems is proud to offer an industry-leading limited lifetime warranty on all our aluminum boat docks.

Why we can offer it

What stands behind the warranty

Four things, on every dock that leaves our shop.

  • High-grade materials

    We use only high-grade aluminum and premium stainless-steel hardware in every dock.

  • Rigorous quality control

    We perform rigorous quality control checks throughout dock production.

  • Precisely built

    Every boat dock is precisely designed, engineered and fabricated.

  • 20+ years behind it

    There is over 20 years of experience behind every dock we build.

More than construction

Your dealer is there before, during and after

It is more than just construction that makes it unlikely you will ever need our warranty.

Your professional dealer representative is there before, during and after your dock is installed, guaranteeing that it is correct in every detail. From the basics of choosing the right dock to details like permits, zoning and access, your dealer makes sure that your dock is right the first time, and for a lifetime.

No other dock manufacturer offers a longer or more complete warranty, or more durable and complete support.

Please contact us or your dealer for more information on the best aluminum dock, and the best boat dock warranty, available anywhere.
