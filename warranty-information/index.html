Warranty Information

A limited lifetime warranty on residential docks installed by Flotation Systems or an authorized dealer.

Coverage

What the warranty covers

The dock is built to last, and the warranty is written to match.

Residential docks installed by Flotation Systems or an authorized dealer carry a limited lifetime warranty on the structure. Roof metal carries a 40-year limited paint warranty and a 40-year color retention warranty.

Flotation billets are covered by our exclusive limited lifetime warranty, and powder coated finishes meet AAMA 2604 certification.

At a glance

The terms in short

Full warranty documents are available from the factory or your dealer.

    Limited lifetime warranty on residential installations.

  • Roof metal

    40-year limited paint warranty and 40-year color retention.

  • Flotation

  • Finishes
