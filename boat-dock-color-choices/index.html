Eighteen roofs, seven railings, four decks

We are proud to offer eighteen beautiful and durable roof colors, seven standard railing colors, and four deck color choices to perfectly match your home or make a distinctive statement.

Custom color choices are available for railings, frames, ramps and posts, using the Sherwin Williams RAL Colors collection — see Powder Coated Finishes below. Finishes

Powder coated finishes

Our finish is the beginning of beautiful, lasting color.

Powder coat provides a deep, colorful finish without compromise, in an almost endless variety of colors and textures. Custom color configurations are available for railing, frames, ramps and posts using the Sherwin Williams RAL Colors collection. Decking and roof metal are not available in custom colors — see the options above.

Electrostatic powder coating results in coverage up to and often exceeding four times that of conventionally applied wet paint, consistent and free from common wet paint flaws. Unlike paint, powder is free from volatile organic compounds and polluting solvents, for a more environmentally-friendly product.

We contract with PowderTec, Inc. for the highest architectural powder coat finish available, meeting AAMA 2604 certification.
