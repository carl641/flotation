Boat Dock Options

Watercraft lifts

If you have a boat or PWC, the ease and safety of a lift is essential.

For stationary docks, we can provide mounting and materials for overhead lifts, installed when your dock is constructed on-site. For floating docks, we offer the even weight distribution of tank-style lifts that connect at four points inside the slip.

Personal watercraft lifts are available as overhead lifts for stationary docks and drive-on lifts for floating docks. Each installation is different, based on the brand and style of lift you select — your dealer will help you choose the right one.

Slip Fill-Ins

Convert an unused slip into useful space for seating or personal watercraft storage.

More detail

Used in multi-slip docks that only need one slip now, a fill-in maximizes your space while keeping the dock ready until the slip is needed.

Fill-ins are available for any size Flotation Systems slip and are custom made to suit your application. The slip can be entirely enclosed, or space can be left — as a 45-degree “V” or square end — for smaller watercraft docking.

Each fill-in looks and acts as part of the dock, featuring the same LockDry® decking as the regular platform. They can be installed with the dock or later, and removed when you are ready to add a boat. They are not intended for repeated installation and removal.

WearDeck™ & Lumberock™

The look of traditional wooden decking, without the maintenance of real wood.

More detail

WearDeck™ is a woodgrain, slip-resistant composite that is splinter-free and resists mold, mildew, pests, stains and fading. Designed to reduce heat absorption, it stays cool and comfortable for bare feet and cleans with soap and water. Eight colors available.

Lumberock™ Coastal Collection decking is engineered with an HDPE and mineral-fill blend for strength and durability, and resists rot, mold, mildew and insects across docks, bridges, gangways, decks and marinas.

LockDry® aluminum decking remains our standard. See your dealer for details.

Boat Dock Accessories

Boat protection, reinvented

Cover your boat in 60 seconds or less with the Marine Concepts boat protection system.

Our team measures and models your boat using state-of-the-art digital tools to craft a one-of-a-kind cover unique to your vessel, ensuring a precise fit for a tighter seal and a cleaner boat. The cover connects to a patented track system so it glides on and off like pulling a curtain.

It works without power, so it is always dependable, safe and easy — no more snapping, crawling or sweating. Installation is tailored to your dock, using clamps instead of screws to avoid permanent damage. Available in Basic Beige, Charcoal Gray, Black Onyx, Atlantic Blue and Slate Gray.
