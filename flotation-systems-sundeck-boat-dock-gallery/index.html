Sundeck boat docks

A second story over the water — marine-grade aluminum decking above the slips, with a limited lifetime warranty.

Every dock in this gallery started as a conversation about one waterfront: its depth, its elevation, and how the family wanted to use the water.

More about sundeck docks→
  • Single, double, triple, and side entry layouts
  • Full sundecks, double decks, and “L” shapes
  • Hip, gable, and lean-to shades on the upper deck
  • Cable, picket, and Beaumont Series railings
  • LockDry® aluminum or WearDeck composite decking
  • Upper- and lower-level ramp entry
  • Spud pole, cable/winch, and follow-the-water anchoring
  • Dock lockers, storage closets, and super cushion bumpers
  • Super swings, slides, Aquastair ladders, and PWC ports
