Fixed & stationary docks

Built on pilings for stable water levels — marine-grade aluminum, with a limited lifetime warranty.

Every dock in this gallery started as a conversation about one waterfront: its depth, its elevation, and how the family wanted to use the water.

More about fixed & stationary docks→
  • Piling-mounted fixed dock structures
  • Stationary sundecks and sundeck combos
  • Stationary frame walkways with split handrails
  • Hip and gable shades with vented decorative ridges
  • LockDry® aluminum or WearDeck composite decking
  • Access ramps and stairs to floating platforms
  • Picket handrails and safety railings
  • Storage closets and dock lockers
  • Fixed walkways for marina approaches
