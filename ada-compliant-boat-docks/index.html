ADA Compliant Docks

We believe that everyone should have access to life on the water.

Get a Quote ADA compliant gallery

Access built into the dock, not added on

From that belief comes our commitment to designing and engineering the finest docks compliant to the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) requirements.

Docks with exceptionally easy and safe access, from shore to dock and from dock to watercraft. Grab bars, toe rails, transition plates and gentle-slope walkways are part of the structure rather than fittings bolted on afterward.

Flotation Systems has installed dozens of ADA compliant boat docks on public waterways and private shores, each specifically tailored to meet the law and the needs of all visitors.

Materials & methods

Built into every ADA compliant dock

The same marine-grade aluminum and stainless steel hardware as every other dock we build.

  • Shore to dock

    Gentle-slope walkways and transition plates at each end, so the route from the bank stays continuous.

  • Dock to watercraft

    Boarding arranged so getting into the boat is as accessible as reaching the dock.

  • Grab bars and toe rails

    ADA style handrails with grab bar and toe rail, powder coated in your color.

  • Tailored to the site

    Each dock specifically tailored to meet the law and the needs of all visitors.

  • LockDry® or open decking

    Watertight LockDry® decking or an open aluminum plank surface, both firm and slip resistant.

  • Marine-grade aluminum

    Corrosion-resistant aluminum with stainless steel hardware. Nothing to rust or rot.

Configurations

Ways to build accessible access

Public waterways and private shores, at whatever elevation the shoreline gives you.

Fishing piers

Covered or open piers with custom ADA fishing rails, public or private.

Floating platforms

Platforms that ride the water level, so the step from walkway to deck stays constant.

Community and courtesy piers

Longer runs with split safety handrails, kayak launches and paddle board racks.
