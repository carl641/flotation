Sundeck Combo Boat Docks

Deck space and dock roof, together. A traditional sundeck with the protection of a dock roof — twenty extra feet of space, covered.

Get a Quote Sundeck combo gallery

Open deck and covered slip, together

The sundeck combo takes the best of a traditional sundeck and combines it with the protection of a dock roof.

You gain 20 extra feet of dock space plus a sloped dock roof that keeps you, your boat, and all your outdoor items safe from the elements.

Sun where you want it, shade where you need it — without giving up either the upper deck or a covered slip.

And speaking of safety, our sundeck combo docks surpass all national building codes. We craft them from waterproof LockDry® aluminum marine decking and powder coated aluminum fencing, making them as safe and durable as they are attractive.

When you add dock options like lockable gates, corner benches, cable railing and custom waterslides, your sundeck combo boat dock will soon become everyone’s favorite escape on the water.

Materials & methods

Built into every sundeck combo

The same materials and construction that go into every Flotation Systems dock.

  • Sloped dock roof

    26-gauge aluminum roof metal with a durable Valspar coating and a long color retention warranty, sloped to shed rain and sun.

  • Twenty extra feet

    The combo layout adds roughly 20 feet of usable dock beyond a covered slip alone.

  • LockDry® aluminum decking

    Watertight interlocking planks with a concealed gutter system keep the lower dock dry.

  • Picket fencing

    Powder-coated aluminum picket fencing around the open deck, color-matched to decking and roof.

  • Cantilevered stairs

    Stairs cantilever over the water so the dock surface stays clear.

  • WAVEPro™ technology

    Stabilizing construction engineered for heavy wakes and rough weather.

Configurations

Ways to configure a combo

Where the roof stops and the open deck starts is yours to decide.

Covered slip, open deck

Roof over the slip, sundeck alongside — the classic combination and the most popular layout.

Double slip combo

Two side-by-side slips under roof with the sundeck carried across the top.

Combo with PWC slip

An offset personal watercraft slip tucked in beside the covered slip, with shaded seating.
