Sundeck combo boat docks

A covered slip and an open sundeck in one dock — marine-grade aluminum throughout, with a limited lifetime warranty.

Every dock in this gallery started as a conversation about one waterfront: its depth, its elevation, and how the family wanted to use the water.

More about sundeck combo docks→
  • Double, triple, and four-slip layouts
  • Shed roofs off each side of the upper deck
  • Hip and gable shades with vented decorative ridges
  • Cable, picket, and Beaumont Series railings
  • LockDry® aluminum or WearDeck composite decking
  • Upper deck ramp connections and stairs
  • Spud pole and cable/winch anchoring
  • Storage closets, dock lockers, and super cushion bumpers
  • Super swings, Aquastair ladders, boat lifts, and PWC ports
