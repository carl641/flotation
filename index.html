We build more than boat docks.

A Flotation Systems dock is your escape: the place where you reconnect with family, laugh with friends, or relax in soothing silence.

Jimmy Conn founded Flotation Systems in 1992 and introduced the first residential all-aluminum boat dock system. Three decades later, every dock still carries that engineering-first approach: marine-grade aluminum framing, stainless steel hardware, and construction that will not rust or rot.

Residential docks installed by Flotation Systems or an authorized dealer are backed by a limited lifetime warranty.

WAVEPro™ Technology

Every Flotation Systems dock features our stabilizing WAVEPro™ engineering, the next-generation answer to high-traffic lakes and extreme weather. When wakes and storms move the water, your dock stays steady underfoot.

Wave attenuation

Engineered assemblies absorb and dampen wake energy instead of passing it into the dock.

Marine-grade aluminum

Corrosion-resistant structural alloy that will not rust or rot, season after season.

Stainless steel hardware

Precision-engineered connections that hold tight through years of movement.
