About Us

Three decades of all-aluminum engineering, starting with the first residential all-aluminum boat dock system.

Jimmy Conn founded Flotation Systems in Cullman, Alabama in 1992. Every dock still carries that engineering-first approach: marine-grade aluminum framing, stainless steel hardware, and construction that will not rust or rot, installed nationwide through our network of authorized dealers.

Residential docks installed by Flotation Systems or an authorized dealer are backed by a limited lifetime warranty.

LockDry® Marine Decking

Watertight aluminum decking that keeps the space under your dock dry and usable.

Interlocking planks channel rain and spray away through a concealed gutter system, with no exposed fasteners to work loose and nothing to splinter, rot, or need refinishing.

The next-gen solution to high traffic lakes & extreme weather conditions

Every Flotation Systems’ dock features innovative, stabilizing WAVEPro™ technology.

“Wave Attentive Versatility Engineered” was developed to skillfully master the waves and stand strong against the forces of nature. Through state-of-the-art engineering and meticulous construction practices, our docks are built to not only survive, but to thrive when conditions get rough.

Flotation Systems’ docks define the dock industry standards for beauty in design, construction, durability and strength — for a lifetime of enjoyment.

Wave attenuation

Engineered assemblies absorb and dampen wake energy instead of passing it into the dock.
